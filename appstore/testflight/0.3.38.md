New look: Miami. Pick Miami as the dark theme (Settings → Appearance) and, in dark mode, the chat, the Agents list, the machines and Settings sit on a violet-to-amber sunset gradient with see-through cards, and the terminal gets matching colors. A Miami app icon can be picked in Settings → App Icon.

Images Claude looks at in Code Mode (screenshots it opens) now show in the chat.

In the demo, picking an answer to the Codex question works again.

What to test
Choose Miami as the dark theme with the phone in dark mode: check that the chat, the Agents list and Settings are readable on the gradient, including a picked answer on a question card. Switch the app icon to Miami and back. In a Code Mode session, ask Claude to look at a screenshot and check that it appears in the chat.
