New in 0.3.17

- Shake your phone anywhere in Bergr to open Diagnostics (switch it off in Settings → Help).
- Report a Problem in Diagnostics: send by email with the log attached, or open a GitHub issue (public: check the log first).
- Settings: new Help, Support and About groups, and clearer icons.
- Chat: messages already sent no longer stay marked "Queued"; long multiple-choice questions show as buttons again; no more "Connecting…" flash on brief drops; a guard against the chat bouncing.
