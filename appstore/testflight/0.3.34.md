If "Reading the conversation…" shows for more than 30 seconds, chat now says it can't read the conversation and offers Retry, instead of spinning forever. Retry starts reading again from scratch. A stall also leaves a line in the app's log saying where it stopped.

What to test
Open a Claude session's chat and check that the conversation loads as before. If you ever see "Can't read the conversation", tap Retry and check that the conversation appears.
