New in 0.3.23

- One setup for every Bergr on your phone: the dev build and this TestFlight build share your machines, their host keys and your SSH keys, so there's no second setup and no extra key to add to your machines. Open the build you set up first once, then this one.
- Settings > Machines: Export Machines and Import Machines move your machines (and their host keys) to another phone or a fresh install. Private keys never leave the device: on another phone, add that phone's key to your machines.

What to Test
- Open your older Bergr once, then this build: your machines should be listed and connect without a new key.
- Export machines, delete one, import the file: it should come back and connect.
