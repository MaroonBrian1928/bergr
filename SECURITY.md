# Reporting Security Issues

We take security bugs in Bergr seriously. We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings, and will make every effort to acknowledge your contributions.

To report a security issue, please use the GitHub Security Advisory ["Report a Vulnerability"](https://github.com/MaroonBrian1928/bergr/security/advisories/new) page. Please don't open a public issue for it.

This covers the app and its code in this repository, for example how it stores SSH keys, verifies host keys or handles what a server sends. Problems in herdr-core itself belong to [herdr](https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr), and problems in Ghostty or Citadel to those projects.

We will send a response indicating the next steps in handling your report. After the initial reply to your report, we will keep you informed of the progress towards a fix and full announcement, and may ask for additional information or guidance.
