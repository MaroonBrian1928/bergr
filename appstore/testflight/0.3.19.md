New in 0.3.19

- Show Chat keeps what you typed: half a line typed in the terminal moves into the chat's reply box and is cleared from the terminal, so your next reply starts on a clean line.
- Keystrokes that may not have arrived after a dropped connection show a bar with Resend and Discard, instead of being lost or sent twice.
- Predict Typing (Settings): what you type shows right away, before the machine echoes it back.
- Diagnostics shows how long keystrokes take to appear on screen.
- Chat: running sub-agents are listed under the working line at the bottom, sub-agents show what they're doing, and code blocks no longer overlap the sub-agent row.

What to Test
- Type part of a command in the terminal, tap Show Chat: the text should be in the reply box and gone from the terminal.
- Turn Airplane Mode on mid-typing, then off: check the Resend/Discard bar and that nothing is sent twice.
- Turn on Predict Typing over a slow connection and check typing feels instant and corrects itself.
- Start an agent with sub-agents and check they show under the working line with detail.
